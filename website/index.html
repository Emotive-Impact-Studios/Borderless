How it works

Three moves. No cables, no buttons, no taking turns.

Everything in the room speaks the same language. If you can drag a window or flick a photo, you already know how to use it.

Laptop to wall

Over the edge

Move a window to the top of your screen. The edge lights up in your colour, the window dissolves on your laptop and reassembles on the wall. It stays a real, live window you keep working in.

Phone to wall

Flick it up

Pick a photo, PDF or file on your phone and flick it towards the screen. It leaves your hand and lands where you aimed, ready for anyone to open.

Wall to phone

Send it back

Anyone at the wall can send a file down to one person's phone. It arrives saved, so the notes leave the meeting with the people who need them.

Shared, at the same time

The big screen becomes a table everyone can use.

Screen sharing hands the room to one person at a time. With Borderless, a colleague adds the spreadsheet beside the deck without interrupting it, and every window carries its owner's colour and name.

Rooms

One system, dressed for the room it is in.

The same Wall, phone and laptop apps work in a boardroom, a lecture hall, a studio or an operations centre. Each room picks a look; the gestures never change.

Whiteboard look

Every seat can reach the board.

A student circles the answer on your slide from their seat. Hands go up before anyone draws, quizzes land on every phone at once, and you can compare answers side by side.
